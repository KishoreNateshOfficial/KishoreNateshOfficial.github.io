MyPostiz

A personal video scheduling tool by Kishore Natesh

What it does

MyPostiz is a private tool used by its owner to schedule and publish his own gaming videos (short clips) to his own YouTube and Instagram channels. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

How it uses Google / YouTube

MyPostiz asks the channel owner to sign in with Google so that it can upload videos to, and read basic information about, the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses this access only to publish the owner's scheduled videos. It does not access anyone else's account or data.
