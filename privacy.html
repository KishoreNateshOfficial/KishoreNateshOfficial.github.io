Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

MyPostiz ("the app") is a private video scheduling tool operated by Kishore Natesh ("I", "me"). It is used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own social media accounts.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token for the owner's YouTube channel, plus basic profile information (channel name and email address). The app uses YouTube permissions to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to read basic channel information.

How the information is used

The tokens are used solely to publish the owner's scheduled videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The app does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the owner's own content.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and are not transferred to any third party. Access to the server is restricted to the owner.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the app. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can ask for any stored data to be deleted by contacting me.
